02 · Fixes

What changed after the audit

Each pair shows the files before and after the fix, on paper, where the problems are easiest to see. The edits to the artwork live in tools/patches.py; the original files are untouched.

03 · Motion studies

Eight motions, each for a specific job

Every study stays under 800 ms, eases out, and lands on the baseline without drift. Select a card to replay it with a new value. The playback speed above applies here too.

04 · Treatments

One geometry, six finishes

The finishes reuse the facet shading. The metal and pearl sets map each facet's luminance through a duotone ramp, so the light direction stays top-left in every version. Select a tile to catch the light.

05 · Handoff spec

Timing and easing tokens

Durations are at 1× speed. Stagger runs left to right across digit places, so the eye reads the number the same way it reads text.
